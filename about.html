My journey

I’m currently studying at 42 Paris, where I’m developing strong foundations in C, Unix environments, memory management, algorithms and collaborative problem solving.

My interests are broader than a single technology. I’m especially drawn to artificial intelligence and products that connect software to physical devices.

Rather than presenting every tool as something I have already mastered, I use projects to document what I am learning. My goal is to become capable of designing complete systems: from low-level code and secure backends to accessible interfaces and hardware prototypes.

Calista AI is the main product vision I want to develop over time. Neko will be integrated into it as a connected companion concept. Alongside that work, I plan to experiment with Arduino, ESP32 and Raspberry Pi.
